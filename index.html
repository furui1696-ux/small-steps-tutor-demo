A few things you might
be wondering.

Who are these lessons for?+

This sample offering is for students aged 12–18, from maths foundations to school test preparation. A parent or guardian would arrange lessons for anyone under 18.

What would I need for an online lesson?+

A laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection, paper, and a pen. A real tutor would share the meeting link and confirm any materials in advance.

Can I book a real lesson here?+

No. This is a portfolio demo with a fictional tutor. The form only previews a request in your browser. It does not send messages, save your details, or reserve a lesson.

How much do lessons cost?+

No lessons are sold through this demo, so no payment is requested. On a live site, lesson prices and cancellation terms would be provided before you book.
